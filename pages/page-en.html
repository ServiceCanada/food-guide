The colours offered by this theme aim to match the branding of Canada's food guide which uses maroon as a primary colour and several other secondary colours.

Sponsor: Alan Carter on behalf of Health Canada - Web publishing team.

Accessibility statement: These colours meet the colour contrast requirements as outlined in WCAG 2.1 AA Success Criterion 1.4.3: Contrast (Minimum) and where validated using the Colour Contrast Analyser program. I can certified that the color matches used in this thematic are meeting a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and also a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 for graphics and user interface components. I did my due diligence and to knowledge and from my understanding, all elements of this thematic are meeting WCAG 2.1 AA standards. Tested by Alan Carter, alan.carter at hc-sc.gc.ca. 2026-02-20.

Progressive enhancement notes:

  • The bg-cfg-maroon, bg-cfg-aqua and bg-cfg-aqua-dark classes must be accompanied with another dark contrast background colour such as bg-dark and with text-white colour to ensure sufficient contrast between text and background.
  • The bg-cfg-beige, bg-cfg-aqua-light and bg-cfg-yellow classes must be used with standard text colour to ensure sufficient contrast between text and background.
  • The .btn-cfg-maroon, .btn-cfg-aqua and .btn-cfg-beige button classes must be accompanied with a fall back button class such as .btn-default or btn-primary.
  • The .panel-cfg-maroon class must be accompanied with a fall back color such as panel-default.

Other styles

Canada's food guide theme .cfg

This style can be used on a container to influence the styling of it's contents to adopt the Canada's food guide themed maroon colour on various elements outlined below.

H1 header tag

Applies a maroon underline under the standard H1 title of the page. This can be applied to either the <h1> or the <hgroup> containing the stacked H1.

Example

The example has been applied to the <h1> element of this page.

Code
<h1 id="wb-cont" class="cfg">Canada's food guide (CFG) theme</h1>

<hgroup id="wb-cont" class="cfg cfg-print">
	<p>Canada's food guide</p>
	<h1>Baked blueberry and banana oatmeal</h1>
</hgroup>

List group items

Overrides the default colour on the active list-group-item to a maroon background.

Example

Pagination

Adds the Canada's food guide maroon colour to the active button in the pagination at the bottom of a list of items. The class must be applied on the parent container of the container that has .wb-paginate applied to it.

Print styles .cfg-print

Canada's food guide contains sections that are designed to be printed out in a printer friendly brochure format such as recipes that can fit on 2 printable pages and require customized print styles to replace PDF versions. These styles format the H1 header title block using a different format that aligns with their print media and shrinks the print size and margins on some elements.

This section can be tested by going to "Print preview" in your current web browser.

Header block .cfg-print

Applying the .cfg-print class to the <hgroup> containing the <h1> title will apply the Canada's food guide print style for it. If the section is related to the Canada's food guide kids section which uses a different colour scheme then we can add the class .cfg-kids to change this.

Examples:

Canada's food guide

Baked blueberry and banana oatmeal

Canada's food guide kids

Toolkit for educators: Activities for ages 4 to 6

    <h1>Baked blueberry and banana oatmeal</h1>
</hgroup>

<hgroup id="wb-cont" class="cfg cfg-kids cfg-print">
    <p>Canada's food guide <span>kids</span></p>
    <h1>Toolkit for educators: Activities for ages 4 to 6</h1>
</hgroup>
